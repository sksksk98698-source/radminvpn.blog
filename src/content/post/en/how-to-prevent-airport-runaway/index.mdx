Exit scams in the proxy industry stem from unsustainable financial architectures where fixed monthly bandwidth leases outstrip new subscriber acquisition.

### 1. The Economics of Failure

Operating private IEPL circuits demands rigid monthly commitments. Ultra-cheap multi-year promotions create negative margins, invariably resulting in abandonment once reserve cash is depleted.

### 2. Five Critical Red Flags

- Unscheduled steep discounts (e.g., 50%+ lifetime renewals).
- Muted community groups and disabled Telegram comments.
- Ticket support queues exceeding 48 hours without human resolution.
- Domain unreachable for over 72 hours without official mirror links.
- Prolonged node outages falsely attributed to upstream maintenance.

### 3. Emergency Playbook

Export active offline node configurations, file immediate payment dispute claims via your processor, and rotate credentials to prevent database credential stuffing.
